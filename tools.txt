6. Video presentation analysis tools
This is the video analyzer module you want to build.

One important distinction: don't claim to measure someone's actual confidence, honesty, or personality from facial expressions, eye contact or posture. These are unreliable signals. Build presentation-quality feedback instead.
Tool name	Responsibility
analyze_face_visibility	Check whether the face is visible
analyze_camera_framing	Check camera positioning and framing
analyze_lighting_quality	Detect poor lighting
analyze_video_quality	Check blur, resolution and frame quality
analyze_head_pose	Estimate head orientation
analyze_posture	Analyze observable posture and positioning
detect_person_in_frame	Check whether the candidate is in frame
detect_multiple_persons	Identify multiple people in the frame
analyze_background	Identify visual distractions
generate_video_presentation_feedback	Generate actionable presentation tips

Keep these as coaching metrics, not candidate-selection scores. Obtain consent and provide an option to disable video analysis.
